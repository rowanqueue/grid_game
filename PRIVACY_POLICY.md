## Overview

Floracotta is a single-player puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account. This policy describes what data the app stores on your device and how third-party services may collect data when you use the app.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs and local files. This data is not synced to our servers:

- **Game progress** — in-progress save files
- **Seeds (in-game currency)** — balance and earn timers
- **High scores** — local leaderboard entries
- **Settings** — music volume, sound volume, dice mode, haptics preference
- **Tutorial progress** — whether you completed the tutorial and certain lesson flags
- **Difficulty** — selected difficulty and unlock state

You can delete this data by uninstalling the app or using in-app restart flows where available.

## Third-party advertising

Floracotta uses **Unity Ads** to show optional rewarded video advertisements. When you choose to watch an ad, Unity may collect device and usage data as described in Unity’s privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not receive your personal identity from Unity Ads. Rewards (seeds) are granted only after a completed ad view.

## Data we do not collect

- No user accounts or registration
- No cloud save or cross-device sync in v1.0
- No location access
- No contacts, photos, or microphone access

## Children

Floracotta is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
