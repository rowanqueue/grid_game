## Full description

Plant your way to victory in Floracotta — a relaxing grid puzzle where every tile you place shapes your garden and your score.

**How to play**
- Draw tiles from your bag and place them on the grid
- Match colors and numbers to merge tiles and unlock new ones
- Use tools like the spade, watering can, and clipper to solve tricky spots
- Fill the board strategically — when there’s no room left, your run ends

**Features**
- Satisfying merge chains and score pops
- In-game seed economy with optional rewarded ads
- Tool shop, bag view, snapshots, and mulligan
- Guided tutorial for new players
- Local high scores across difficulty levels
- Music and sound controls, optional haptics

No account required. Play at your own pace.
