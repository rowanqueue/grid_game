APP STORE LISTING TEMPLATE
==========================

Everything Google Play and the Apple App Store ask for, in one place.
Fill in anything in [BRACKETS]. Character limits are in (parentheses).
Limits and image sizes change now and then, so check them against each
store's current help pages before uploading.


SHARED BY BOTH STORES
=====================

APP ID (permanent on both stores, can never change)
------
Reverse domain style, such as com.[company].[app]. Use the company name if a
company will own the app.

  [APP ID]


APP NAME (30)
--------
  [APP NAME]


CONTACT
-------
  Support email          [EMAIL]          (both stores, required)
  Support URL            [URL]            (Apple, required)
  Marketing URL          [URL]            (Apple, optional)
  Website                [URL]            (Google, optional)
  Privacy policy URL     [URL]            (both stores, required, even if the
                                           app collects nothing)


PRIVACY POLICY
--------------
A hosted page anyone can open. It should cover what data the app collects,
why, who it's shared with, and how to get in touch. If the app collects
nothing, say so plainly. It must match the answers in Google's Data safety
form and Apple's App Privacy label.

  Collects data?         [YES / NO]
  What and why           [LIST, OR "NOTHING"]
  Third-party SDKs       [ADS / ANALYTICS / CRASH REPORTING / NONE]


DESCRIPTION (4000, both stores)
-----------
The first two or three lines matter most: both stores cut the text off there
until the reader taps "more". Write for the device: "tap" and "drag", not
"click" or keyboard keys.

  [OPENING HOOK, 1-2 LINES]

  [WHAT THE PLAYER DOES]

  [FEATURES, AS A SHORT LIST]

  [OPTIONAL: CREDITS OR A CLOSING LINE]


CATEGORY
--------
  Primary                [CATEGORY]
  Secondary              [CATEGORY]       (Apple: Games can take two
                                           subcategories)


SCREENSHOTS (shot list)
-----------
Pick portrait or landscape and keep every screenshot the same way. The first
two or three show in search results, so lead with the strongest. Captions or
frames around the shots are allowed on both stores.

  1. [SHOT]
  2. [SHOT]
  3. [SHOT]
  4. [SHOT]
  5. [SHOT]


VIDEO (optional)
-----
  Google                 A YouTube link, any length. Ads must be off on it.
  Apple                  App previews: up to 3, 15-30 s each, uploaded as
                         files. Must show footage captured from the app
                         itself.

  [VIDEO PLAN OR LINK]


RIGHTS AND CREDITS
------------------
  [ ] Written agreement with every contributor (code, art, music, sound)
      allowing commercial release by whoever owns the app
  [ ] Licenses for third-party art, fonts, sounds and code allow commercial
      use, and their notices ship inside the app
  [ ] Any jam, contest or publisher terms the project was made under allow
      releasing it here
  [ ] App name is not already taken on either store, and not a trademark


GOOGLE PLAY
===========

TEXT
----
  App name (30)              [APP NAME]
  Short description (80)     [ONE LINE]
  Full description (4000)    see DESCRIPTION above


IMAGES
------
  App icon                   512 x 512 PNG, 1 MB max. Google adds the rounded
                             mask, so fill the square.
  Feature graphic            1024 x 500 JPG or PNG with no transparency.
                             Required. Keep important text away from the
                             edges, it is sometimes cropped.
  Phone screenshots          2-8. Short side 320-3840 px, long side no more
                             than twice the short side. At least 4 at 1080 px
                             or more to be eligible for featuring.
  7" tablet screenshots      Optional. Needed to be featured on tablets.
  10" tablet screenshots     Optional. Same as above.
  Chromebook screenshots     Optional.

POLICY FORMS (Play Console > App content)
------------
  [ ] Privacy policy URL
  [ ] App access             Does any part need a login? If so, give
                             reviewers a test account.  [ANSWER]
  [ ] Ads                    Contains ads?  [YES / NO]
  [ ] Content rating         IARC questionnaire: violence, language, gambling,
                             user interaction, etc.
  [ ] Target audience        Age groups the app is for. Choosing any group
                             under 13 brings in the Families policy (stricter
                             rules on ads, data and SDKs). Google may also ask
                             whether the app could appeal to children.
                             [AGE GROUPS]
  [ ] Data safety            What is collected and shared, matching the
                             privacy policy.
  [ ] News, health, financial, government declarations  (usually "no")

APPLE APP STORE
===============

TEXT
----
  App name (30)              [APP NAME]
  Subtitle (30)              [SUBTITLE]       Shows under the name in search.
  Promotional text (170)     [TEXT]           Top of the description. Can be
                                              changed any time without review.
  Description (4000)         see DESCRIPTION above. Changing it needs a new
                             version.
  Keywords (100)             [WORD,WORD,WORD]
                             Commas, no spaces. Don't repeat words already in
                             the name or subtitle, they count anyway.
  Copyright                  [YEAR] [OWNER]
  What's New (4000)          [RELEASE NOTES]  (not needed on the first version)


IMAGES
------
  App icon                   1024 x 1024 PNG, no transparency, square
                             corners (iOS rounds them). Goes in the Xcode
                             asset catalog, not uploaded separately.
  iPhone screenshots         6.9" display: 1320 x 2868 or 1290 x 2796
                             (portrait; swap for landscape). 1-10 shots.
                             Apple scales them down for smaller iPhones.
  iPad screenshots           13" display: 2064 x 2752 or 2048 x 2732.
                             1-10 shots. Required only if the app runs on
                             iPad. Make the app iPhone-only to skip them.
  App previews (optional)    Up to 3 per device size, 15-30 s.

FORMS (App Store Connect)
-----
  [ ] Age rating             Questionnaire: violence, mature themes,
                             gambling, user-generated content, etc.
  [ ] App Privacy            "Data Not Collected", or each data type with
                             its purpose, matching the privacy policy.
  [ ] Encryption             Most apps answer "no" (only standard HTTPS or
                             none). Set ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption to
                             false in Info.plist to skip the question on
                             every upload.
  [ ] EU trader status       Digital Services Act. A company is a trader:
                             its address, phone and email show publicly on
                             the EU store.
  [ ] Content rights         Does the app use third-party content? Do you
                             have the rights to it?
  [ ] App Review info        Contact name, phone, email, notes for the
                             reviewer, and a demo account if there's a login.
                             [NOTES]


BUILD AND ACCOUNT
-----------------
  [ ] Developer account      Individual or organization ($99/year).
                             Organization needs a D-U-N-S number.
  [ ] Mac with Xcode, or a cloud build service, to build and upload
  [ ] Bundle ID              matches APP ID above
  [ ] Version / build        [VERSION] / [BUILD]
  [ ] TestFlight             Test on real devices before submitting


BEFORE SUBMITTING
=================
  [ ] Every [BRACKET] filled in
  [ ] Privacy policy is live and matches both stores' privacy forms
  [ ] Screenshots taken from the release build, all one orientation
  [ ] Description reads right for touch (no mouse or keyboard words)
  [ ] Tested on a real Android phone and a real iPhone
  [ ] Tested on the smallest screen the app supports, and on a notched one
  [ ] Android back button does something sensible
  [ ] App pauses when sent to the background, and audio stops
  [ ] Saved data survives closing and reopening the app
  [ ] Links inside the app open somewhere the player can get back from
  [ ] Rights and credits checklist done
